Questions

What ages is this for?

Star Quest is designed for children aged 6–9. Younger kids (5) can play with adult help reading. Older kids (10) may find it easy but still enjoy the treasure hunt format.

Do I really not need a printer?

Correct. The read-aloud mode lets you read each riddle from your phone or tablet, then walk together to the answer location. No cards to cut or hide.

How long does it take?

Setup takes about 10–15 minutes (hiding the cards). The hunt itself takes 20–35 minutes depending on the child's age and whether you give hints.

What if my apartment is small?

Every riddle answer is a common household item found in any home: a pillow, a book, shoes, a chair. No garden, garage, or multiple floors needed.

Can I use it for more than one birthday?

Yes. Print new cards or use read-aloud mode again. The riddles don't change, so you may want to save it for siblings or playdates rather than repeating for the same child.
