Free Printable Birthday Treasure Hunt for Kids

A ready-to-play indoor treasure hunt with five riddles, a parent guide, and a completion certificate. Ages 6–9. Under 15 minutes to set up.

If you need a last-minute birthday activity that works in a small home or apartment, Star Quest gives you everything in a single HTML file. No apps, no accounts, no licensed characters, no child data collected.

What You Get in the Free Sample

  • 6 riddle cards — five clues plus a final message card
  • Completion certificate — write the child's name in by hand
  • Parent hiding guide — exact locations for each card, with safety notes
  • No-printer option — read clues aloud if you don't have a printer
  • A4 and US Letter — works in any country
  • Low-ink print mode — clean black-on-white when you hit Print
Why "zero-printer"? Unlike most printable treasure hunts, Star Quest works fully without a printer. Open the file on your phone or tablet, read each riddle aloud, and place the next clue as described. No cutting, no ink, no paper.

How It Works

  1. Download the free HTML file (works offline, no internet needed during play)
  2. Read the parent guide — takes 3 minutes
  3. Hide cards 2–6 at the locations shown (cushion, book, shoes, table, chair)
  4. Hand card 1 to the child and let them solve each riddle
  5. Celebrate when they bring you the final card

Who Is This For?

  • Parents planning a birthday party at home (apartment or house)
  • Anyone who needs a same-day activity with zero prep time
  • Families who don't own a printer (use the read-aloud mode)
  • Groups of 1–8 kids, ages 6–9

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a printer?

No. Star Quest includes a read-aloud mode. Open the HTML file on your phone or tablet and read each riddle to the child. They show you the answer, and you place the next clue.

What ages is this for?

The riddles are written for ages 6–9. Younger children (5 and under) may need an adult to read the clues. Older children (10+) may find the riddles too easy.

How long does it take to set up?

About 10–15 minutes. You need to cut out the cards (if printing) and hide 5 cards at the locations shown in the parent guide.

How long does the treasure hunt last?

Most children finish in 10–20 minutes, depending on age and group size.

Is it safe for small children?

All hiding spots are low, reachable, and away from electrical appliances and heights. An adult must be present for the entire hunt. The parent guide includes safety reminders.

Can I use it outdoors?

The free sample is designed for indoor use. A future version with garden-friendly locations is planned.

Is this really free? What's the catch?

Yes, the sample is free. A 12-clue full version with personalised names, alternate routes, and an answer key is planned as a paid upgrade. The free sample is complete and usable on its own.
