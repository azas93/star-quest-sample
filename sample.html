📋 Setup — Parent Guide

Print this page and the riddle cards (pages 2–4). Cut out the nine cards along the card borders. You will need scissors.

🗺️ Where to Hide Each Card

CardRiddle AnswerHide It Here
Card 1 (hand to child)Cushion— Starting point, not hidden
Card 2BookUnder a couch cushion
Card 3ShoesInside a book on a shelf
Card 4TableNext to shoes near the front door
Card 5ChairOn the kitchen or dining table
Card 6Washing machineOn the seat of a sturdy chair
Card 7RefrigeratorOn top of or beside the washing machine
Card 8MirrorInside the fridge, on a shelf
Final Card (★)Grown-upTaped to the bathroom mirror
Treasure (adult gives)🎉 Surprise!Hand to child after the final card

🚀 How to Run the Hunt

  1. 1Cut out all nine riddle cards from pages 2, 3, and 4.
  2. 2Hide cards 2 through 9 at the locations shown above. Do not hide card 1.
  3. 3Prepare a small treasure (candy, stickers, a little toy) in a box or bag.
  4. 4Hand card 1 to the child and ask them to read the riddle out loud.
  5. 5Guide the child without giving away answers. Cheer them on!
  6. 6When the child brings you the final card, celebrate and give them the treasure!
⚠️ Safety: Never hide a card inside an electrical appliance, up high, or anywhere hard to reach. Make sure every hiding spot is safe and age-appropriate. An adult must be present for the entire hunt.
